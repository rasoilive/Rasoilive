PRIVACY POLICY

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Rasoi Live ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

1.2 This policy is drafted in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1.3 By accessing or using our platform, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "User" refers to any individual or entity that accesses or uses the Rasoi Live platform.

2.2 "Personal Information" means any information that relates to a natural person, which, either directly or indirectly, in combination with other information available or likely to be available, is capable of identifying such person.

2.3 "Platform" refers collectively to the Rasoi Live website and any associated mobile applications or services.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

3.1 Personal Information: We may collect identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and billing address when you register an account, place an order, or contact us.

3.2 Payment Information: For transactions, we collect billing details. Note that sensitive card data is processed directly by our secure Payment Gateway Providers and is not stored on our servers.

3.3 Device & Usage Data: We automatically collect information about your device (IP address, browser type, operating system) and how you interact with our Platform (pages viewed, time spent, links clicked).

4. COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

4.1 We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze trends, and administer the website.

4.2 You can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level, but choosing to disable cookies may limit your use of certain features or functions on our Platform.

5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

5.1 To process and fulfill your orders, including managing payments, shipping, and returns.

5.2 To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.

5.3 To improve our Platform, products, and services through data analytics.

5.4 To send administrative information, such as updates to our terms and policies.

5.5 To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraudulent activities.

6. DATA SHARING & THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

6.1 Payment Gateway Providers: We share necessary transaction data with authorized payment processors to facilitate secure payments.

6.2 Analytics: We use third-party tools like Google Analytics to understand Platform usage. These providers may collect information sent by your browser as part of a web page request.

6.3 Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

7. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

7.1 We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7.2 While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure; therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. DATA RETENTION

8.1 We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

9. USER RIGHTS

9.1 Access & Update: You have the right to access and update your personal information through your account settings.

9.2 Deletion: You may request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal obligations we may have to retain specific records.

9.3 Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw your consent for data processing at any time, which may affect your ability to use certain features of the Platform.

10. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

10.1 Our Platform is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information immediately.

11. CHANGES TO POLICY

11.1 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

11.2 Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on the Platform with an updated "Last Updated" date.
