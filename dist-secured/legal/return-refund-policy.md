# RETURN & REFUND POLICY

**Last Updated: February 16, 2026**

## 1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 At Rasoi Live, we strive to ensure customer satisfaction. This Return & Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which returns and refunds are processed for products purchased on our platform.

## 2. RETURN ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
2.1 To be eligible for a return, the following conditions must be met:
   - The request must be initiated within **[Insert Days, e.g., 48 hours / 7 days]** of delivery.
   - The item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition as received.
   - The item must be in its original packaging with all tags and labels intact.
   - Proof of purchase (invoice/order ID) must be provided.

## 3. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
3.1 Certain items are non-returnable due to hygiene, safety, or perishable nature, including:
   - Perishable food items and beverages.
   - Custom-made or personalized products.
   - Items on clearance or "Final Sale."
   - Opened or used personal care products.

## 4. REFUND PROCESSING TIMELINE
4.1 Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
4.2 If approved, the refund will be processed to your original method of payment within **[Insert Days, e.g., 5-7]** working days.
4.3 Please note that the time taken for the amount to reflect in your account may vary depending on your bank or card issuer.

## 5. CANCELLATION RULES
5.1 Orders can only be canceled before they have been dispatched.
5.2 Once an order is in "Shipped" or "Out for Delivery" status, it cannot be canceled.
5.3 If a cancellation is accepted, any prepaid amount will be refunded as per the timeline mentioned in Section 4.

## 6. LATE OR MISSING REFUNDS
6.1 If you haven’t received a refund yet, first check your bank account again.
6.2 Then contact your credit card company/bank, as it may take some time before your refund is officially posted.
6.3 If you’ve done all of this and still have not received your refund, please contact us at rasoilivee@gmail.com.

## 7. SHIPPING COSTS FOR RETURNS
7.1 If the return is due to an error on our part (wrong item sent, defective product), Rasoi Live will bear the return shipping costs.
7.2 For other returns (change of mind, size issues where applicable), the customer may be responsible for paying the shipping costs for returning the item.
