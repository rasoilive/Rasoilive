Getting Started

From Sign-Up to Live
in Under an Hour

We handle the setup. You focus on the food.

I
Register Your Restaurant

Fill out the registration form with your restaurant details — name, location, number of tables, and contact info. Our team reviews and contacts you within 24 hours to complete onboarding.

II
We Set Up Your Branded Platform

Satyronix sets up your personalized instance of Rasoi Live — with your restaurant name, logo, and initial menu pre-loaded. We hand you your owner credentials and table QR codes, ready to print.

III
Print QR Codes → Place on Tables

Print the unique QR codes for each table on any printer. Laminate them, frame them, or stick them on the table — that is your entire hardware setup. Zero cost, zero complexity.

IV
Go Live — Instantly

The moment your first customer scans a QR code and places an order, you're live. Watch orders stream in real-time on your dashboard. Serve faster, earn more, and close the day with an automatic revenue report.

Satyronix Publications ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Rasoi Live platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when registering your restaurant or using our services. By using Rasoi Live, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

When you register your restaurant, we collect:

  • Restaurant name, cuisine type, and address
  • Owner's full name, phone number, and email address
  • Number of tables and operational details
  • Pricing expectations and special requirements
  • Date and time of form submission
3. How We Use Your Information
  • To contact you for onboarding and setup of your Rasoi Live platform
  • To customize your restaurant's digital identity and menu system
  • To provide technical support and service updates
  • To send invoices and manage your subscription
4. Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored securely in a private, access-controlled database managed by Satyronix Publications. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Only authorized team members have access to your data, strictly for operational purposes.

5. We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Your data belongs to you and is used solely to operate and improve your Rasoi Live experience.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your restaurant is active on our platform or as required by law. Upon cancellation of your subscription, you may request full deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights
  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by contacting us
8. Cookies

Our registration website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools that share data externally. Basic session functionality may use cookies solely for site operation.
